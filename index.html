Questions

Good questions.

Do my friends need the app too?

Yes. Each person has Pact on their own phone. You join the same crew with an invite link.

Does the alarm work on silent?

Yes. Pact uses Apple's alarm system (iOS 26), the same one the Clock app uses.

Does the camera record me?

No. Your phone counts push-ups on the spot. Nothing is saved or uploaded.

Is it on Android?

iPhone first. Join the waitlist and pick Android. That tells us how many people want it.
